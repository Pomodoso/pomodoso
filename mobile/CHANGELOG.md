# Changelog

## v1.0.1 (In progress)

### Getting started

- **Welcome screen** — On first launch, choose to start with a few sample tasks and habits or a clean slate, or sign in / restore a backup if you already use Pomodoso. Choosing a clean slate keeps it clean: the samples don't come back.

### Apps

- **New Apps tab** — Browse the app directory and install apps: Quit smoking, Kanban, Bookshelf, Year in Pixels and more. Each opens with all its screens, forms, charts and boards, and its own settings. Signed out, apps open read-only.
- **Apps on Today** — Apps that offer it show a small card on Home: tap + on a counter, pick today's state, type the page you're on.
- **Apps can ask a quick question** — During a break, one app may ask something like "Did you smoke during this break?"; apps that track your days ask about yesterday. Every question can be dismissed.
- **Apps sync** — With sync, your apps and their entries follow you between the phone and the extension.

### Meetings

- **New Schedule tab** — All of today's meetings, tracked or not. Choose which show on Home (today only or always), start one with the stopwatch, or log its time once it's over.
- **Meeting detail** — Notes and description with formatting, a project, time logged, and the notes of the last times a recurring meeting happened.

### Tasks

- **Formatted descriptions** — Bold, italic, lists and code in a task's description, with a preview.
- **Task order under All workspaces** — Each workspace keeps the order you set, here and in the extension.

### Habits

- **Paused challenges are locked** — A challenge waiting on a missed day can't be ticked until you keep going or start over, like in the extension.

### Data

- **Complete backups** — Exports now include meetings, achievements and apps, and leave out this device's own sync state, so restoring on another phone works cleanly and syncs what it restored.
